Privacy is a principle

Designed to reveal less.

A future product should collect only what is necessary, explain why it is needed, and protect it with clear retention and access rules.

  • No public legal names in browsing views
  • No real identity documents stored in this demo
  • No tracking pixels or third-party analytics in this file
  • No messaging, booking, or payment workflow
Non-negotiables

Consent. Safety. Transparency.

Any real service needs an age-appropriate access policy, meaningful consent, a reporting and response process, secure data handling, and legal advice tailored to the exact service and jurisdiction.

Do not submit real names, phone numbers, identity documents, or sensitive personal information through this prototype.

Common questions

Before anything goes live.

Does this demo connect customers and providers?

No. There are no profiles, introductions, messaging, bookings, payments, or meetup features.

Can I use real personal information to test it?

Please don't. The demo does not need real personal information and is not a secure verification system.

What should happen before launch?

Obtain legal advice on the exact service model and jurisdiction, define age and consent safeguards, create privacy and data-retention policies, and conduct a security review before enabling any real user data or contact functionality.
